  <h1>Francisco Javier Blanco Romero</h1>
  <p class="affiliation">Ph.D. student in Telematics Engineering at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid</p>
  <p class="areas">Cryptography · Quantum technologies · Networking · Distributed systems · IoT</p>

I studied Physics at Universidad Complutense de Madrid and earned an M.Sc. in Robotics from Universidad Miguel Hernández de Elche.

Before starting my Ph.D., I worked at [Etelätär Innovation](https://etelatar.com/) as a researcher, technical manager, and software engineer, primarily on IoT systems.

I am a member of the Pervasive Computing Laboratory in the Telematic Engineering Department at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. My research focuses on cryptography, quantum and post-quantum security, secure network protocols, randomness, and distributed systems.
